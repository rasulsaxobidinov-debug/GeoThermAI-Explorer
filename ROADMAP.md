# GeoThermAI Explorer Roadmap

## Vision

GeoThermAI Explorer aims to become an intelligent decision-support platform for geothermal resource exploration by integrating Artificial Intelligence, geospatial technologies, and scientific data analysis.

Throughout development, the project will emphasize:

* Transparency

* Security by design
* Scalability
* Maintainability
* High-quality documentation
* User-centered design

# Current Focus

The immediate priority is completing the MVP required for the National Artificial Intelligence Startup Competition while establishing a solid technical foundation for future development.
