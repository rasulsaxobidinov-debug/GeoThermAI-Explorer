## Overview

GeoThermAI Explorer is an Artificial Intelligence platform designed to support geothermal resource exploration by integrating geological, geophysical, geothermal and satellite datasets into a unified decision-support system.

The platform applies machine learning and geospatial analysis to identify promising geothermal areas, estimate exploration potential, reduce geological uncertainty and support data-driven decision making.

The project is being developed as an AI-powered platform for researchers, government agencies, energy companies and investors interested in sustainable geothermal development.

---

## Vision

Our vision is to build an intelligent digital platform capable of accelerating geothermal exploration while reducing exploration costs and uncertainty through Artificial Intelligence.

---

## Mission

To provide an AI-driven decision support platform that enables faster, more reliable and more sustainable geothermal resource assessment.

---

## Why GeoThermAI Explorer?

Traditional geothermal exploration requires years of field surveys, multidisciplinary interpretation and significant financial investment.

GeoThermAI Explorer combines multiple data sources into one intelligent platform capable of producing fast preliminary assessments using AI-assisted analysis.
